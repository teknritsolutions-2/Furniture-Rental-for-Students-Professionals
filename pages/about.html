Circular Lifecycle

The End of Disposable Fast-Furniture

Cheap flat-pack furniture often warps after a single monsoon or cracks during a stairwell move, inevitably ending up dumped on curbs or classified resale apps at 80% losses.

MODULIV operates on a closed circular model: we design and manufacture furniture using durable solid European beechwood, cold-rolled aircraft steel, and water-repellent commercial upholstery engineered for 30+ tenancy lifecycles.

Between each lease, our technicians disassemble, steam-sanitize, re-coat surfaces with organic beeswax, and tighten every bolt — delivering showroom-fresh comfort to every new tenant.

Hygiene Guarantee

The 7-Step Multi-Point Refurbishment Protocol

Every piece of furniture undergoes a hospital-grade restoration before arriving in your home.

01

Logistics Intake Inspection

Detailed 18-point photographic scan checking joinery, drawer glides, mattress firmness, and veneer integrity upon warehouse return.

02

140°C Thermal Steam Extraction

Commercial deep extraction steam treatment eliminates 99.9% of bacteria, dust mites, and allergens from mattresses and sofas.

03

Torque & Hardware Realignment

Every bolt, cam lock, and bed slat bracket is retightened to calibrated torque specifications with industrial vibration-dampening washers.

04

Zero-VOC Timber Buffing

Solid wood tabletops receive micro-sanding and a fresh coat of organic food-safe beeswax oil to restore natural satin wood grain.

05

Fabric Stain Shield Reapplication

Eco-friendly fluorocarbon-free hydrophobic spray shields upholstery against accidental liquid spills and coffee drops.

06

Quality Assurance Sign-Off

Senior fulfillment supervisor inspects all pieces and attaches a physical QA verification certificate before route packaging.
