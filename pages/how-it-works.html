The Rental Experience

How Renting with MODULIV Works

Moving to a new city shouldn't mean buying disposable flat-packs or hauling heavy trucks. We have engineered an effortless 4-step rental journey that adapts as your life evolves.

🛋️

1. Curate Room

Pre-matched sets designed for modern floor plans.

⚡

2. 48h Delivery

Verified postal corridors with chosen time windows.

🛠️

3. White-Glove Setup

Zero tools or labor needed. Technicians assemble all.

🔄

4. Swap & Move

Change styles, relocate, or return with 1 click.

Step-by-Step

From Empty Room to Complete Home

01

Choose Your Curated Room Package

Browse our Starter, Essential, or Premium bundles tailored for compact student bedrooms, work-from-home focus pods, or full 1BHK/2BHK flats. Compare the included pieces and sample dimensions on each package page.

  • ✓ Pick pre-matched pieces: No mismatched colors or awkward heights.
  • ✓ Choose 3, 6, or 12 months: Rates adjust dynamically with longer stays.
02

Check PIN Code & Select Delivery Slot

Enter your 6-digit postal code across Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Pune, Chennai, or Mumbai. Select a delivery window that aligns with your apartment lease commencement.

  • ✓ 48 to 72-hour turnaround: Staged and routed rapidly from regional depots.
  • ✓ Weekend and evening slots: Book around your work or college schedule.
03

White-Glove Delivery & Assembly

Our trained technicians arrive, transport items directly into your designated rooms, bolt frames securely, adjust leveling feet, and wipe down surfaces.

  • ✓ Zero manual labor for you: No flat-pack confusion or missing allen keys.
  • ✓ Clean finish: All protective corrugate, wrap, and pallets are cleared away.
04

Manage Everything in Your Customer Dashboard

Open your digital dashboard to track active subscriptions, view next monthly due dates (the 15th of each month), initiate item swaps, submit early-return notices, and download sample PDF agreements and payment receipts.

  • ✓ Itemized billing statements: Perfect for corporate expense claims.
  • ✓ One-click swap workflow: Submit replacement requests without customer service hold queues.
On-Site Logistics

What Happens on Delivery Day

From the morning dispatch notification to the final handover, here is what you can expect.

Morning Dispatch SMS

You receive a 2-hour arrival window with lead driver contact and vehicle registration for gated apartment security.

Careful Room Staging

Technicians lay protective floor runners and assemble beds, study tables, and sofas in your designated bedrooms and living areas.

Zero Waste Clean-Up

Every carton, strap, and protective quilt is packed into the vehicle, leaving your newly furnished home sparkling clean.

Living On Your Terms

Adapt Your Space Whenever Life Changes

Your subscription evolves as you do — no trapped contracts.

Mid-Term Style Swaps

Swap Items Without Leaving Home

Moved from remote work to hybrid and need to swap your large study desk for an extra lounge armchair? Submit a swap request in your portal; we deliver the new item and retrieve the old in a single synchronized visit.

Included free on 6 and 12-month subscriptions.
Vacation Lease Hold

Pause Billing for Summer Holidays

College students going home for summer semester breaks or professionals on extended overseas client assignments can pause monthly billing for up to 45 days while securing their active room inventory.

Simple 1-click hold request in customer dashboard.
Smooth Transitions

When Your Tenancy Nears Completion

No last-minute panic or classified ad distress sales. Choose between three simple paths.

Path 1: Extend Tenancy

Enjoying your flat? Extend with a single tap in your dashboard and lock in lower monthly loyalty rates.

Path 2: Relocate Within City

Moving to another neighborhood? Our team moves your furniture to the new address for free on 12-month plans.

Path 3: Schedule Return

Pick a pickup date. Our technicians disassemble and haul away. Your deposit is refunded to bank within 48 hours.

Closed Ecological Loop

What Happens When Furniture Returns?

Unlike consumer retail where returned items end up in scrap piles, every MODULIV item is welcomed back into our circular refurbishment center.

Our craftsmen treat each piece with 140°C thermal steam sanitization, apply food-safe organic beeswax to restore natural timber luster, and replace any high-wear hardware parts.

This circular stewardship reduces household waste by 85% compared to purchasing fast furniture.
