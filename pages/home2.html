Lifecycle Timeline

How Your Subscription Evolves

Your rental isn't a locked contract. It's an adaptable service designed around your life changes.

1

Month 0: Immediate Turnkey Move-In

Your order is scheduled within 48 to 72 hours. Technicians arrive, assemble every item, wipe down surfaces, and take away all packaging boxes.

2

Month 3–6: Swap or Refresh Anytime

Need an extra desk or want to upgrade your 2-seater sofa to a sectional? Submit a swap request from your customer dashboard with zero restocking penalties.

3

Month 12+: Free Annual Deep-Clean & Renewal Discounts

Longer tenures unlock complimentary upholstery shampooing and reduced monthly renewal rates.

4

Exit / Relocation: Zero Hauling Stress

Moving out? Submit your preferred pickup date. We collect the items and reconcile your deposit within 5 to 7 business days.
