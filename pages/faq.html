Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about leases, security deposit refunds, delivery corridors, maintenance coverage, and customer portal workflows.

1. Leases, Tenures & Security Deposits

We offer three standard subscription durations: 3 months (short stays & internships), 6 months (college semesters & project stints), and 12 months (best monthly savings). Tenures can be extended seamlessly anytime via your customer dashboard.
The deposit is calculated at 1.5× your monthly subscription rent. It is held in an escrow balance and refunded via UPI or direct bank transfer within 48 hours following physical pickup and clearance inspection.
Yes. You can initiate an Early Return Request in your customer dashboard with 14 days advance notice. Early settlements reconcile the difference to the shorter tenure rate without punitive termination fees.

2. Delivery, Assembly & White-Glove Logistics

Inside standard metropolitan corridors (Hyderabad, Bengaluru, Pune, Chennai, Mumbai), white-glove delivery and assembly are scheduled within 48 to 72 hours of executed agreement signing.
Every item undergoes our 7-step refurbishment protocol: 140°C thermal steam sanitization, joinery realignment, and natural organic beeswax buffing. Items arrive in showroom-grade condition.
Never. Our trained logistics technicians handle everything: room-of-choice placement, frame bolting, leveling, and complete removal of all protective transport wrap.

3. Cleaning, Maintenance & Accidental Wear

Normal everyday living friction, minor surface scratches, and light fabric spots are 100% covered under our fair wear-and-tear policy. Zero deductions are made for standard living usage.
Yes. Subscribers on 6+ month plans can request a free in-home maintenance visit every 6 months to tighten bed frame bolts, re-level desks, or deep-shampoo sofa upholstery.
Simply submit a maintenance request with a quick photo through your portal. Our local technician visits within 24 hours to repair the mechanism or swap the item free of charge.

4. Swaps, City Relocations & Vacation Holds

Subscribers on 6+ month plans can request a free furniture swap. Select your current item in the dashboard, choose the replacement piece, and pick a date. We deliver and collect in a single synchronized trip.
Yes! Students on semester plans can pause billing for up to 45 days during summer vacations while retaining their package booking without termination penalties.
We offer free relocation assistance for 12-month subscribers. Our team disassembles your furniture at the old address, transports it, and reassembles it in your new flat.

5. Customer Portal, Invoicing & Tax Documents

All tax invoices and digital rental agreements are permanently stored and downloadable as signed PDFs inside your Customer Dashboard under the "Billing History" tab.
Yes. We support corporate invoicing with corporate GST details, purchase order mapping, and direct corporate net-30 billing accounts for relocating employees.
We accept all major UPI apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), credit and debit cards, net banking, and automatic monthly recurring standing instructions (e-NACH).
