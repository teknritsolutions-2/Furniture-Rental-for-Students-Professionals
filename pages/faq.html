Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about leases, security deposit refunds, delivery corridors, maintenance coverage, and customer portal workflows.

1. Leases, Tenures & Security Deposits

We offer three standard subscription durations: 3 months (short stays & internships), 6 months (college semesters & project stints), and 12 months (best monthly savings). Tenures can be extended seamlessly anytime via your customer dashboard.
The demo calculates deposits at 1.5× monthly rent. No deposit is collected or refunded here; actual conditions would require a confirmed agreement.
Yes. Life changes happen. You can initiate an Early Return Request in your portal with 14 days advance notice. Early settlements reconcile the price difference between shorter tenures transparently without punitive hidden fees.

2. Delivery, Assembly & Furniture Condition

The coverage checker shows sample delivery estimates for five cities. These are not live availability or dispatch promises.
The concept is built around reuse and care. Exact furniture condition and preparation standards would need confirmation before a real rental.
Care and damage coverage shown in this prototype are sample terms. Actual exclusions, fees and cleaning services would need confirmation. Structural replacement is only assessed in cases of intentional mutilation.

3. Swaps, Billing & Customer Portal

Subscribers on 6+ month plans can request an item swap directly inside the customer dashboard. Simply choose your current piece, pick your desired replacement, and provide a preferred date. Our team handles collection and replacement in a single trip.
Once logged in, open the "Documents" or "Billing" tab in your portal. You can download official vector PDF copies of your signed rental agreements and itemized GST payment receipts with a single click.
Monthly rental payments fall due on the 15th calendar day of each ongoing billing month, with automated reminder notices delivered to your registered email and portal notifications.
