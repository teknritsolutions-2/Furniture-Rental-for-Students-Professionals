What Every Delivery Includes

  • ✓ Room of Choice Placement: Technicians place every piece precisely where you want it.
  • ✓ Complete Assembly & Leveling: Beds assembled, table legs bolted, desk grommets fitted.
  • ✓ Zero Waste Haul-Away: Cardboard boxes, bubble wrap, and straps are removed and recycled.
  • ✓ Staircase Carry: Included up to 4th floor when building lacks a service elevator.

Building Access & Operational Boundaries

  • • Society Gate Approvals: Resident must arrange entry permission for delivery trucks with apartment security.
  • • Elevator Dimensions: King-size solid wood headboards require standard 8-passenger elevator or open stairwell clearance.
  • • Narrow Alleyways: Locations unreachable by 14ft medium trucks require extended route coordination.
