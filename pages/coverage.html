Clear Prep Checklist

Delivery Day Move-In Checklist

Three quick checks to ensure your delivery proceeds smoothly and on time.

1. Society Move-In Permission

Check whether your residential gated society requires a Move-in NOC or security deposit deposit receipt before delivery vehicles arrive.

2. Doorway & Hallway Clearances

Standard 1BHK/2BHK flat entry doors (32"+ width) accommodate all MODULIV flat-pack components before final room assembly.

3. Room Layout Planning

Ensure your bedroom or living room floor area is clear of luggage and previous items so our technicians can assemble rapidly.
