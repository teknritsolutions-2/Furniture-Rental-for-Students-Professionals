Standard Rental Terms & Covenants

Last Updated: October 2026 • Specimen v2.4

1. Lease Tenure & Recurring Subscriptions

All furniture packages are leased for defined terms of 3, 6, or 12 calendar months. Rental cycles begin upon physical delivery and verification of assembly. Monthly rental payments are automatically debited on the 15th calendar day of each recurring month.

2. Security Deposit Reconciliation

A refundable security deposit equal to 1.5x of the monthly rental rate is held during the active lease tenure. Upon end-of-tenure return and quality inspection, the full deposit balance is returned within 5 to 7 business days to the customer's registered bank account.

3. Care Guarantee & Wear and Tear

MODULIV covers all standard wear, minor fabric scuffs, and normal usage degradation under our care covenant. Accidental spills can be treated via our complimentary scheduled upholstery cleaning. Structural damage resulting from willful alterations or unauthorized disassembly is assessable at repair cost.

4. Swap & Early-Return Protocols

Customers on 6-month or 12-month tenures may initiate item swaps or early return requests through the customer dashboard with 14 days notice. Swaps are executed without restocking penalties subject to regional corridor availability.

5. Digital Documentation & E-Signatures

Official PDF agreements generated in the customer portal provide complete itemizations and serial numbers. Sample watermarked agreements are rendered for client review and verification.
