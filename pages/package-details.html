Rental Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about tenures, deposits, and delivery.

How is the security deposit calculated?

The refundable deposit is 1.5x your monthly subscription rate. It is held securely and returned to your original bank account within 7 business days following item return inspection.

Can I extend or shorten my rental tenure?

Yes. You can extend your subscription at the same discounted monthly rate directly from the customer dashboard. For early terminations, a modest adjustment to the standard shorter tenure rate applies.

What happens if I move to a new apartment in the city?

We provide one free intra-city relocation per year for customers on 6-month or 12-month tenures. Our team will disassemble, move, and reassemble everything at your new address.
