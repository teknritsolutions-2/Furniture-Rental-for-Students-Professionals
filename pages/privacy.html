Data Governance

Demonstration Privacy Policy

Complete privacy standards and transparent local data handling on MODULIV.

Last Updated: October 2026 • Specimen v1.8

Privacy Details

1. Client-Side Data Handling

MODULIV is structured as a static frontend application capable of client-side demonstration without requiring an active database backend. Any profile inputs, swap forms, or sample invoices generated remain strictly inside your browser's local sandbox.

2. Absence of Financial Harvesting

Per strict engineering standards, this application never prompts for, transmits, or logs genuine payment cards, bank CVVs, or government identity tokens. All transaction previews simulate billing states for UI walkthrough purposes only.

3. LocalStorage Reset Capabilities

Users may wipe all simulated active rentals, swap requests, and payment statements at any time by clicking the "Reset Demonstration Data" action inside the Customer Dashboard Settings.
