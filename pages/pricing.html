Clear Rental Policies

How Deposits, Swaps & Returns Work

Refundable Security Deposit

Deposits are pegged at 1.5x of your monthly rental rate. When your tenure concludes and items are inspected for basic structural integrity, 100% of your deposit is returned to your original payment account within 5 to 7 business days.

Item Swap Policy

Need to change a sofa color or swap a single bed for a queen bed? Customers on 6+ month plans can submit a swap request directly through the customer dashboard. We coordinate collection of the existing piece and delivery of the new item in one trip.

Early Return Process

If your company relocates you or your semester ends early, you can request pickup with 14 days notice. Any tenure adjustments or early settlement terms are calculated transparently without punitive charges.
