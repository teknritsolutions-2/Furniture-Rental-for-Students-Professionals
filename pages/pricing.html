Clear Finance

The 100% Refundable Security Deposit Process

No opaque deductions or weeks of chasing property managers. Here is our straightforward escrow protocol.

Step 1

Initial Escrow

A standard deposit equivalent to 1.5× monthly rental is collected at digital agreement signing and held in an interest-neutral tenant escrow balance.

Step 2

In-Lease Protection

Normal surface friction, microscopic scuffs, and natural timber settling are 100% forgiven under our everyday living policy without deposit penalties.

Step 3

Instant 48-Hour Refund

Our pickup technician executes a 3-minute app-based clearance check. Your deposit refund is initiated directly to your bank account or UPI within 48 hours.
